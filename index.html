The Blue Necklace

A Sherlock Holmes interrogation

About 3 minutes · Sound on · Typing works, voice is optional

Voices by ElevenLabs

The case

Saturday night, the Ashford house in Mayfair. During Lady Mary's dinner party, her blue necklace vanished from her locked study.

James has been her butler for twenty years. He says he served dinner all evening. He is proper, loyal, and quietly devoted to his grown son, Peter.

He has come to Baker Street to answer your questions. Get the truth out of him.

How to win

  • Get James to confess within 8 turns.
  • Pressure rises when you confront him with evidence that matters. Click an item on the desk, then Present to James.
  • Trust rises when you show you understand why a good man might do a bad thing.
  • He confesses only when Pressure and Trust are both high and he feels understood.
  • Insult him (thief, liar) and he shuts down. Do it twice, or lose his trust entirely, and he walks out.
  • Not every clue points where it seems to.

Controls

  • Type and press Enter, or hold Space / Hold to talk to speak (Chrome, Edge).
  • Move the mouse to the screen edges, or drag, to look around.
  • Hold right-click or Lean In to study his face.
  • Stuck? Click Watson's chair, to your right, for a hint.
